## WHAT WE DECIDED

After researching India's biggest unsolved problems and evaluating 10+ startup ideas, we chose:

### Business: AI Digital Presence for Indian MSMEs

**The problem:** 63 million Indian MSMEs (clinics, CAs, tutors, salons, boutiques, gyms) have zero online presence. No website, no Google listing, no WhatsApp catalog. They lose customers daily to competitors who just have a basic Google page. Agencies charge ₹15,000–₹50,000/month — completely out of reach.

**The solution:** An AI platform that builds a complete digital presence for any MSME in under 10 minutes — from 10 simple inputs. Website + Google My Business listing + WhatsApp Business catalog + monthly social media posts. Auto-generated by AI. No tech skills needed.

**One-liner:** "Your entire digital business — built in 10 minutes, for less than a cup of chai per day."

## KEY DECISIONS & PRINCIPLES ESTABLISHED

1. **Start with clinics** — highest willingness to pay, clearest pain point
2. **CA partner program is the main distribution moat** — prioritize above all else
3. **No physical visits** — all outreach via Apify scrape + WhatsApp DM
4. **Template system not custom builder** — ship in 3 weeks not 3 months
5. **Speak outcomes not features** — "234 people found you" not "your SEO is improved"
6. **Demo is the pitch** — the live 30-second website generator closes the sale
7. **GMB verification takes up to 14 days** — set expectation in onboarding, don't promise instant
8. **Time to first result = churn killer** — user must see a Google review, visit, or inquiry in 7 days
